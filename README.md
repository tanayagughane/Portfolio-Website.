## 🚀 Features You’ll Find Here  
- 🧍‍♀️ **About Me** — A short story about my academic journey and passion for technology.  
- 💻 **Skills** — A visual grid of my technical expertise (HTML, CSS, JS, Python, Java, SQL).  
- 🧩 **Projects** — A showcase of some of my favorite works, including my Sentiment Analysis system and mini web projects.  
- 🏆 **Experience & Achievements** — Highlights from internships, hackathons, and awards.  
- 📄 **Resume Download** — You can view or download my resume directly.  
- ✉️ **Contact Form** — Easy way to reach out for collaborations or opportunities.  
- 🌗 **Dark & Light Mode** — Toggle themes according to your mood.  
- 📱 **Fully Responsive** — Looks great on any device, from mobile to desktop.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **CSS3** — for layout, animations, and design aesthetics
